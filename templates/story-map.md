A basic story map of the customer need is the starting point for the [design workshop](../docs/stages/02-design-workshop.md). The product manager and the agent prepare it from the brief's customer workflows: the customer's starting state, the completed opportunity, and the main steps between. The squad fills in everything else together in the room.

This is the text form. If your squad uses a whiteboard tool such as Miro or a Confluence whiteboard, recreate it there with the same rows. The rows matter more than the tool.

## Rules for the backbone
- Written in the customer's terms, as things they do, never as system features
- Starts at the customer's starting state and ends at the completed opportunity, with nothing missing in between
- Every activity and step traces to a customer workflow in the brief
- Every failure point traces to an unhappy path in the brief
