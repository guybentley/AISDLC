# Story map: <title>

**Brief:** <link to the problem brief>  **Prepared by:** <product manager> with the brief-coach  **Status:** empty, ready for the workshop

An empty story map is the starting point for the [design workshop](../docs/stages/02-design-workshop.md). The product manager and the agent prepare the backbone from the brief's customer workflows. The team fills in everything else together in the room.

This is the text form. If your team uses a whiteboard tool such as Miro or a Confluence whiteboard, recreate it there with the same rows. The rows matter more than the tool.

## What is prepared, and what is left empty

| Row | Prepared before the workshop | Filled in by the team |
|---|---|---|
| Activities | Yes, from the customer workflows | The team may rename, add or remove |
| Steps | Yes, in customer terms | The team may change them |
| Stories | **Empty** | Yes |
| When things go wrong | Failure points named, from the brief's unhappy paths | Stories for each |
| Release slices | **Empty** | Yes |
| Annotations | **Empty** | Yes: exists / new / risk / unknown |

Leave the lower rows empty on purpose. A pre-filled map anchors the room and stops people thinking about the customer.

## Rules for the backbone
- Written in the customer's terms, as things they do, never as system features
- Every activity and step traces to a customer workflow in the brief
- Every failure point traces to an unhappy path in the brief
